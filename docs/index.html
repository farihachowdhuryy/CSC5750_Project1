Overview About Myself

Hello everyone! My name is Fariha Chowdhury, and I’m going into my third year studying Computer Science at Wayne State University.

Over the past two summers, I’ve worked as a Rocket Technology Mentor, where I’ve had the opportunity to share my passion for technology and work with students. I’ve also volunteered and completed internships at TechTown as a software developer, helping startups build and grow their businesses through technology.

Looking ahead, my goal is to build a career in the tech industry and eventually earn my master’s degree in Computer Science, with a focus on Artificial Intelligence. I’m excited to continue learning, gaining experience, and exploring new opportunities in the field.

Outside of my coursework, I’m involved in several programs at Wayne State. I’m a member of the Honors College, a Program Assistant for the Academic Success Center, and an Honors Peer Mentor. These experiences have allowed me to connect with other students, support my community, and grow both personally and professionally.

When I’m not studying or working, I love spending time with my friends and checking out new coffee shops. I’m also really interested in art and enjoy making glass mosaics and clay sculptures in my free time. I also love traveling, exploring new places, and spending time in nature.

I’m always excited to meet new people and learn new things!

My Top 3 Favorite TV Shows

Modern Family Modern Family is one of my favorite shows because it is funny and has so many different personalities. Gloria is my favorite character because she is super dramatic and always makes me laugh. I also love how the family is always getting into random and funny situations. The show is really easy to watch, and I never get tired of the characters. My favorite highlight from the show was when Lily goes to a Vietnamese restaurant for the first time.
The Neighborhood I really like The Neighborhood because the characters have such funny relationships with each other. Tina is my favorite character because she is funny and always has something to say. I like watching how the two families become closer even though they are very different. The conversations and jokes between the characters are what make the show so entertaining to me.
The Summer I Turned Pretty I really like The Summer I Turned Pretty because of all the drama and complicated relationships. My favorite part is the relationship between the two brothers and how it affects everyone around them. I like how the story keeps you wondering what will happen next. The love triangle makes the show really interesting, and I always want to watch another episode.
